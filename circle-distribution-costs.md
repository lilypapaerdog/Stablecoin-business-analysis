# Circle Distribution Costs Analysis

## Objective
Understand how distribution costs affect Circle's financial economics.

## Business Model
Circle earns reserve income from assets backing USDC. However, a portion of this income is paid to distribution partners under commercial agreements.

## Illustrative Financial Model
- Annual reserve income: $2.0 billion
- Distribution costs: $1.0 billion
- Revenue remaining after distribution costs: $1.0 billion

## Key Insight
Distribution costs can materially affect the economics of a stablecoin issuer. Growth in USDC circulation does not necessarily translate proportionally into profitability.

## Research Questions
1. How does Circle share USDC economics with Coinbase?
2. How do distribution costs change as USDC circulation grows?
3. What creates a sustainable competitive advantage for Circle?
