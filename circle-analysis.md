# Circle Business Model Analysis

## Company Overview
Circle is a financial technology company and the issuer of USDC, a US dollar-denominated stablecoin.

## Revenue Model
Circle earns revenue primarily from income generated by the reserve assets backing USDC.

## Key Financial Drivers
- USDC in circulation
- Interest rates and reserve yields
- Distribution and partnership costs
- Operating and compliance expenses

## Research Questions
1. How does Circle generate revenue from USDC reserves?
2. How do changes in interest rates affect profitability?
3. What role does Coinbase play in USDC distribution?

## Next Steps
Research Circle's financial disclosures and develop a revenue sensitivity analysis.
