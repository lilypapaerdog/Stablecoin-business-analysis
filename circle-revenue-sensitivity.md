# Circle Revenue Sensitivity Analysis

## Objective
Analyze how changes in interest rates could affect Circle's reserve income from USDC.

## Business Model
Circle earns a substantial portion of its revenue from the reserve assets backing USDC. Revenue is influenced by USDC circulation, reserve yields, and commercial arrangements with distribution partners.

## Simplified Financial Model
Annual reserve income = Average reserve balance × Annual reserve yield

## Illustrative Assumptions
- Average USDC reserve balance: $50 billion
- Base annual reserve yield: 4%
- Sensitivity: ±1 percentage point

## Scenario Analysis
| Scenario | Reserve Yield | Estimated Annual Reserve Income |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lower yield | 3% | $1.5 billion |
| Base case | 4% | $2.0 billion |
| Higher yield | 5% | $2.5 billion |

## Key Findings
- A 1-percentage-point decrease in reserve yield reduces estimated annual reserve income by $500 million, assuming a constant $50 billion reserve balance.
- Reserve income is sensitive to both interest rates and the size of USDC reserves.
- Reserve income is not the same as Circle's net revenue or profit because distribution costs, operating expenses, and other factors must be considered.
